Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026. This policy explains what information Pourly Made Co. ("Pourly Made", "we", "us") collects through pourlymadeco.com and the Pourly Made Pinterest integration, and how it is used.

Who we are

Pourly Made Co. is a small-batch soap and candle business operated by one person in the Spokane, WA area. pourlymadeco.com is a showcase site. It does not sell products online, take payments, or offer user accounts.

Information we collect

Contact form

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you enter. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which forwards the message to our email inbox. We use this information only to reply to you. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone.

Automatically collected information

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking pixels on this site. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operation. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when a page loads.

Pinterest integration

Pourly Made uses the Pinterest API through its own app to publish and schedule Pins on the Pourly Made Pinterest account and to read that account's own Pins, boards, and basic analytics.

  • The app connects only to the Pourly Made Pinterest account, which is authorized by its owner through Pinterest's OAuth sign-in.
  • It does not access, collect, or store data about other Pinterest users.
  • Access tokens are stored privately and securely, are never shared, and are used only to post content and read account data for Pourly Made.
  • Pinterest data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Pinterest's settings under Security & logins, then Apps. Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

Pinterest's own handling of your data is covered by the Pinterest Privacy Policy.

Affiliate links

Some Pourly Made content, including Pins and videos, links to products on Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, Pourly Made earns from qualifying purchases. When you follow one of those links, Amazon may set cookies and collect data under the Amazon Privacy Notice. We do not receive your personal information from Amazon.

How long we keep information

Contact form messages are kept in our email inbox only as long as needed to answer your question. Pinterest access tokens are kept only while the integration is in use.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only with the service providers named above, as needed to run the site, or when the law requires it.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any message you have sent, or ask what information we hold about you, through the contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
