About

Yes, the name is a pun.

Hi, I'm Treena. Pourly Made is small-batch melt-and-pour soap and hand-poured candles, made by one person in the Spokane, WA area. Every batch starts with a pour, and not every pour goes to plan. The misfires get shown, not hidden, because that's how the good batches happen.

The story

From software systems to soap.

I've spent 20+ years as a software systems developer. Soap and candle making is my hobby, and Pourly Made started as a proof of concept built on it.

That background shows up in the process: tested, repeatable base recipes, consistent results, and no shortcuts. The creativity goes into the scents, colors, and designs.

Every product starts from a proven base (goat milk, triple butter, and a lot of beeswax), then gets its own scent, color, and finish.
